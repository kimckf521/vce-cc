## Tier definitions

- **core-drill**: definitional / direct-procedure exercises. Single-step calculation, formula-recall, or simple identification. Past-paper Exam-1 MCQs of the easiest type.
- **extended-fit**: standard application — formula in context, simple multi-step reasoning, a small table or diagram. Most "B"/"C" difficulty exam questions.
- **modelling-rich**: applied/contextual — multi-part problems built around a scenario, table, or diagram. Always involves choosing/interpreting tools rather than executing fixed procedure. Most Exam-2 questions sit here.

VCAA Study Design (VCE Mathematics 2023-2027) emphasises *interpretation in
context* for General — observable in past papers where >70% of Exam-2 questions
reference a stem table/graph/network.

## Style notes per tier

- **core-drill**: short stems, plain prose. Numerical answer, single concept. Distractors = sign-flip / off-by-one / unit confusion. No diagrams unless trivial.
- **extended-fit**: 2-3 sentence stem with small table or labelled figure. Distractors = wrong formula choice / mis-read figure.
- **modelling-rich**: full scenario stem (50-200 words). Embedded table or SVG diagram is the norm. Distractors target common misconceptions (Hamiltonian↔Eulerian, deseasonalise direction, transition matrix steady state).

## Tech classification

- **TECH_FREE**: simple-formula computations (arithmetic seq term, area/volume of regular shape, slope from two points, simple matrix product 2×2). About 35-40% of items.
- **CAS_ALLOWED**: most algebraic/matrix questions where CAS is helpful but not necessary.
- **CAS_REQUIRED**: numerical regression/financial recurrence/Markov-chain-power problems. About 25% of items, predominantly modelling-rich.
